projyct.gotz — Hand Rig Controls

  • 14-Pass CCD IK Drag: Grab any finger tip or distal/middle/proximal bone to curl and aim cleanly into place.
  • Metacarpal Manipulation: Drag any metacarpal bone shaft (or use finger cards) to adjust palm arch, splay, and rotation.
  • Coupled IK: When active, rotating one metacarpal subtly recruits adjacent metacarpals (35%).
  • Wrist Wheel: Drag the rotary dial to rotate the entire hand from the carpal hub.
  • Live Sync: When opened from the main Puppyt figure, every pose update syncs live back onto the figure.
